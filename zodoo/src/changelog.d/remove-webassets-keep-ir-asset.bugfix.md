`remove_webassets` no longer runs `delete from ir_asset` on Odoo >= 17. Since Odoo 15 asset bundles are assembled purely from `ir.asset` records, and those are created from the modules' manifest `assets` key at install/update time - a restart or an admin login does not bring them back. Purging them therefore turned `odoo setup remove-web-assets` (and the restore step, unless `NO_REMOVE_WEB_ASSETS_AFTER_RESTORE=1`) from a cache clear into a broken instance: empty bundles, HTTP 500 on `/web/assets/...`, no login until `odoo update` ran. The generated bundle attachments are now deleted by their url instead. Additionally `odoo filestore unshare` skips databases whose postgres cannot be reached (one postgres container per instance) rather than aborting the sweep.
