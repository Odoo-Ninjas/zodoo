`odoo build --repair-zodoo-registry` repairs a corrupt or stale image in the zodoo (fast-build helper) registry: it rebuilds the image locally (ignoring the registry copy) and overwrites the registry with the fresh build. It bundles the two low-level flags so you don't have to remember the combination, and asks interactively whether to also drop the local Docker build cache (`--no-cache`).

The underlying building blocks are also available on their own: `--force-zodoo-registry-push` (`-ZPf`) re-uploads images to the zodoo registry even when they were pulled instead of rebuilt; `--no-zodoo-pull` (`-ZPl`) skips the registry pull. The SRC_EXTRA and registry-config gates still apply, so customer-baked images are never uploaded.
